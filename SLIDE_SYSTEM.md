# Slide System

## Canvas

Default:

- 16:9 aspect ratio
- Consistent margins
- High resolution assets

## Narrative Structure

Typical research presentation:

1. Title
2. Background
3. Research question
4. Method
5. Key results
6. Mechanism / interpretation
7. Discussion
8. Conclusion

## Visual Priority

Prefer:

- Figures
- Diagrams
- Timelines
- Process flows
- Data visualization

Avoid:

- Dense paragraphs
- Decorative graphics without meaning
- Unsupported claims
