# Research Slides Style Presets

Visual systems for scientific presentations. Each preset defines a complete design language.

---

## Scientific Minimal

**Vibe**

High-impact academic communication for papers, lab meetings, and conferences.

**Layout**

- Figure-first composition
- Generous whitespace
- Precise grid alignment
- Thin structural dividers

**Typography**

- Elegant serif or high-contrast display font
- Clean readable body font

**Colors**

- White background
- Charcoal text
- Muted scientific accents

**Signature Elements**

- Large figures
- Small source labels
- Minimal decoration
- Clear evidence hierarchy

**Avoid**

- Dashboard-like cards
- Decorative graphics without meaning

---

## Research Dark

**Vibe**

Technical, computational, AI-oriented, system-focused.

**Layout**

- Dark canvas
- High contrast figures
- Interface-inspired annotation

**Typography**

- Modern display sans
- Monospace accents for code and equations

**Colors**

- Deep navy / black
- Cyan or green technical accents

**Signature Elements**

- Model pipelines
- Architecture diagrams
- Layered technical annotations

---

## Editorial Academic

**Vibe**

Review articles, interdisciplinary research, and conceptual talks.

**Layout**

- Magazine rhythm
- Narrative pacing
- Strong statement slides

**Typography**

- Serif headlines
- Neutral sans body

**Colors**

- Warm paper tones
- Dark ink
- One restrained accent

**Signature Elements**

- Research story over section transcription
- Carefully selected visual anchors

---

## Data Story

**Vibe**

Statistics, modeling, experiments, and quantitative analysis.

**Layout**

- Visualization-first
- Large readable charts
- Comparison structures

**Typography**

- Strong numerical hierarchy
- Compact annotations

**Colors**

- Neutral base
- Controlled categorical accents

**Signature Elements**

- Every chart answers a question
- Metrics are labeled
- Uncertainty is visible when available

---

## Selection Rule

Choose style based on:

1. Research field
2. Audience
3. Speaker-led or reading-first mode
4. Evidence complexity

Never choose a style only because it looks attractive in isolation.
