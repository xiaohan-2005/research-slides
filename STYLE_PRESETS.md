# Style Presets

## Scientific Minimal

Use for:
- Nature style reports
- Laboratory meetings
- Academic conferences

Characteristics:
- White space
- Clean typography
- Focus on figures

## Research Dark

Use for:
- AI topics
- Technical demonstrations
- System presentations

Characteristics:
- Dark background
- High contrast
- Modern interface feeling

## Editorial Academic

Use for:
- Review presentations
- Interdisciplinary talks

Characteristics:
- Magazine-like layout
- Strong storytelling

## Data Story

Use for:
- Statistical analysis
- Modeling results

Characteristics:
- Data visualization first
- Minimal text
