# Research Rules

## Scientific Integrity

Research Slides follows strict evidence rules:

- Never fabricate citations.
- Never invent numerical results.
- Never modify reported experimental findings.
- Clearly separate source facts from generated explanations.

## Extraction Priority

When available, prioritize:

1. Original paper text
2. Figure captions
3. Tables
4. Supplementary materials
5. User-provided notes

## Figure Rules

Before using a figure:

- Understand what it measures.
- Read its caption.
- Record its source.
- Explain why it supports the narrative.

A visually attractive figure is not automatically a useful figure.

## Quantitative Rules

For every reported number preserve:

- value
- unit
- metric definition
- dataset or experimental condition
- comparison baseline when relevant

If unknown, do not guess.

## Output Check

Before delivery verify:

- Are important claims traceable?
- Are figures correctly attributed?
- Are limitations represented fairly?
- Are equations and units preserved?
- Are interpretations distinguished from reported findings?
