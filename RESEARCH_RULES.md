# Research Rules

## Scientific Integrity

Research Slides follows these rules:

- Do not fabricate citations.
- Do not create unsupported numerical results.
- Do not change reported experimental findings.
- Clearly separate source facts and generated explanations.

## Extraction Priority

1. Original paper text
2. Figure captions
3. Tables
4. Supplementary materials
5. User-provided notes

## Output Check

Before final output verify:

- Are all claims traceable?
- Are figures correctly attributed?
- Are limitations represented fairly?
