Problem

The bottleneck was sequential computation

Recurrent sequence models process hidden states in order. That creates a dependency chain inside each training example and limits parallelization as sequences grow.

x₁token enters recurrent state
h₁must finish before the next state
h₂depends on h₁
h₃depends on h₂
hₙtraining still carries a sequential path of length O(n)

Research question

Can a sequence-to-sequence model remove recurrence and convolution while preserving translation quality—and gain parallelism in the process?

Source: §1 Introduction, especially the discussion of sequential hidden-state computation and the remaining constraint on parallelization.
03 / 12
Core idea

Replace recurrence with attention

“Connect positions by learned relevance, not by stepping through time.”

Interpretation for presentation clarity—not a verbatim quote from the paper.

Global dependency

Each position can attend to relevant positions without distance-dependent recurrence.

Parallel representation

Self-attention over a layer can be computed with matrix operations across positions.

Encoder–decoder retained

The Transformer keeps the transduction structure while changing the internal building blocks.

Source: §1–§3. The paper proposes a Transformer relying entirely on attention to draw global dependencies.

1 · Compatibility

Compute dot products between queries and keys.

2 · Scale

Divide by √dₖ to reduce the risk of large dot products pushing softmax into very small-gradient regions.

3 · Weighted values

Softmax weights determine how the values are combined.

Representation

Multi-head attention: several views in parallel

The base Transformer uses 8 heads, each with dₖ = dᵥ = 64. Each head receives learned projections of Q, K and V; their outputs are concatenated and projected again.

Why multiple heads?

The paper argues that multiple heads let the model jointly attend to information from different representation subspaces and different positions; a single head averages this capacity into one attention map.

Source: §3.2.2. Base configuration: h = 8, dₖ = dᵥ = d_model / h = 64.

Parallelism

Self-attention connects all positions with a constant number of sequentially executed operations.

Long-range paths

The path between any two positions is constant in a full self-attention layer, rather than growing with sequence length.

Source: §4 and Table 1. Complexity advantage over recurrence depends on sequence length n relative to representation dimension d.

12 / 12
Conclusion

What changed—and what remained open

Demonstrated

A sequence transduction model can be built entirely around attention, replacing recurrent layers in the encoder–decoder architecture.

Measured

The paper reports stronger translation results with substantially more parallelizable training than the recurrent/convolutional baselines it compares against.

Still open in 2017

The authors explicitly point to restricted attention for large inputs, non-text modalities, and less sequential generation as future work.

Research Slides principle: separate what the paper showed from what history later made of it.
