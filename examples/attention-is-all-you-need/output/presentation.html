Problem

The bottleneck was sequential computation

Recurrent models generate hidden states in order. Within a training example, later states depend on earlier ones, limiting parallelization as sequence length grows.

x₁input token
h₁state must finish first
h₂depends on h₁
h₃depends on h₂
hₙsequential path grows with n

Research question

Can sequence transduction remove recurrence and convolution while retaining strong translation performance?

Benefit

Full self-attention gives constant sequential operations and constant maximum path length.

Trade-off

Its O(n²d) term matters as sequences become long; the paper explicitly discusses restricted attention as one possible response.

Conclusion

What the paper demonstrated — and what this demo refuses to overclaim

Demonstrated

An encoder–decoder sequence transduction model can be constructed entirely around attention rather than recurrent layers.

Measured

The paper reports strong WMT14 translation quality with a highly parallelizable training architecture.

Open in 2017

The conclusion points to other modalities, restricted attention for large inputs, and making generation less sequential.

Evidence boundary: later historical impact is not treated as a finding of the 2017 paper.
