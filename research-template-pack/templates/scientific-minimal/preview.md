# Scientific Minimal Preview

## Metadata

- Slug: `scientific-minimal`
- Mood: calm, precise, publication-adjacent
- Tone: rigorous, restrained, high-integrity
- Best for: experimental papers, life sciences, engineering, thesis defenses, figure-heavy research
- Evidence focus: source figures, equations, mechanism diagrams, exact comparisons

## Visual Snapshot

A warm-white scientific canvas with editorial serif typography, generous negative space, hairline rules, and one carefully chosen scientific motif. It should feel closer to a beautifully typeset research figure or conference title page than to a corporate presentation template.

## Preview Ingredients

- Background: warm paper `#F8F7F2`
- Ink: near-black `#111418`
- Primary evidence blue: `#315E8A`
- Quiet corroborating green: `#2E7D64`
- Muted slate: `#6B7280`
- Sand/caveat accent: `#B38A54`
- Display typography: `Source Serif 4` / `Georgia` / serif fallback
- Body typography: `IBM Plex Sans` / `Aptos` / `Segoe UI`
- Technical labels: `IBM Plex Mono` / `Consolas`

Signature moves:

- very large serif title with breathing room;
- one restrained line/point/axis/specimen motif;
- thin source rail rather than a heavy footer;
- equations treated as primary scientific objects, never as ordinary inline text;
- annotations placed outside the main evidence object with hairline leaders.

Avoid:

- card grids;
- gradients;
- decorative photography;
- oversized icons;
- dense micro-copy;
- formula strings typed as plain body text;
- tiny source text that becomes illegible in a gallery thumbnail.

## Preview Rules

- Create one real 1920×1080 title slide using actual research content.
- Keep the slide mostly typographic.
- Use at most one scientific motif.
- Preserve a large quiet region around the title.
- Do not render template names, option labels, file paths, or workflow metadata.
- Do not compress a full slide into a tiny preview; design the preview itself to survive thumbnail scale.
- If the title wraps, use deliberate line breaks rather than automatic tight wrapping.

## Gallery Proof Rule

When this style is shown in a public gallery, show three different slide jobs:

1. title / thesis;
2. method, architecture, or equation;
3. result / evidence.

Each thumbnail should communicate one visual idea at a glance. Remove text that only becomes visual noise after downscaling.
