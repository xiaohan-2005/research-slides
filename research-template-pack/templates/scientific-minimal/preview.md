# Scientific Minimal Preview

## Metadata

- Slug: `scientific-minimal`
- Mood: calm, precise, publication-adjacent
- Tone: rigorous, restrained, clear
- Best for: experimental papers, life sciences, engineering, thesis defenses, figure-heavy research
- Evidence focus: source figures, equations, compact tables, mechanism diagrams

## Visual Snapshot

A bright scientific canvas that feels closer to a carefully typeset paper figure than a corporate slide template. Warm-white background, near-black text, muted blue evidence accents, thin rules, and generous whitespace.

## Preview Ingredients

- Palette: warm white `#F7F7F4`, ink `#171717`, slate `#667085`, evidence blue `#315E8A`, quiet green `#2E7D64`
- Typography: Source Serif 4 for major statements, IBM Plex Sans for explanation, IBM Plex Mono for technical labels
- Signature move: one dominant figure or equation with a narrow evidence rail
- Signature move: hairline rules and restrained scientific annotation
- Avoid: card grids, gradients, oversized decorative icons, fake journal branding

## Preview Rules

- Create one real 1920×1080 title slide using actual research content.
- Keep the composition mostly typographic with one restrained scientific motif.
- Do not render template names, option labels, filenames, or workflow metadata.
- The result should look like the opening slide of a serious conference or thesis presentation.
